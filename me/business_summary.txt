Cedar & Crumb Bakery is an artisan bakery and cafe in Mar Mikhael, Beirut, founded in 2021 by head baker Rania Haddad and operations lead Karim Nassar. Our mission is to bake honest, slow-fermented bread and pastries that bring together Lebanese baking heritage and European artisan technique, made from heritage wheat grown by small farmers in the Bekaa Valley and stone-milled in our own bakery every morning. Our tagline is "Mountain grains, city mornings."

We offer five services. Our daily bakery and cafe sells sourdough loaves, man'oushe, croissants, pastries and coffee, with 20 indoor seats and a small terrace. We design custom celebration cakes for birthdays, engagements and weddings, starting at $45 for a 6-inch cake; custom cakes need at least 72 hours' notice and wedding cakes need three weeks and a consultation. We cater corporate breakfasts and events with breakfast boxes ($12 per person) and pastry and mezze platters ($18 per person) for groups of 15 or more, with 48 hours' notice. On weekends we run hands-on baking workshops (Sourdough 101, Lebanese Breads & Man'oushe, and Kids' Cookie Decorating) for up to 10 people. Finally, we offer bread subscriptions: the Crumb Club, a weekly bread subscription for households at $28 per month, and wholesale bread supply for cafes and restaurants, priced by quote.

Our team is small and hands-on: Rania Haddad (co-founder and head baker, trained in Lyon), Karim Nassar (co-founder and operations, a former supply-chain engineer who built our farmer partnerships), Lea Khoury (pastry chef and cake designer), and Omar Saad (workshop lead and community manager).

What makes us different: we are the only bakery in Beirut that stone-mills Lebanese heritage Salamouni wheat in-house every day, every sourdough loaf is fermented for 48 hours, and we bake signature fusion pastries you will not find elsewhere, such as our knefeh croissant. We run a zero-waste kitchen: unsold bread is donated each evening to a local food bank or turned into croutons and bread pudding.

We are open Tuesday to Saturday from 7:00 to 19:00 and Sunday from 8:00 to 15:00; we are closed on Mondays. We deliver within Beirut for a $4 flat fee (free on orders over $50).
